What we do

Everyday medicine for every age, under one roof.

Primary care

Annual physicals, chronic condition management, referrals and the ordinary business of staying well.

Preventive screening

Bloodwork, blood pressure, cholesterol and age-appropriate screening, reviewed with you in person.

Same-day visits

Slots held open each morning for sore throats, sprains, rashes and the things that cannot wait a week.

Paediatrics

Well-child visits, immunisation schedules and growth tracking from newborn through adolescence.

Women's health

Routine gynaecological care, contraception counselling and menopause support.

Travel medicine

Destination-specific vaccination and prophylaxis, ideally four to six weeks before you fly.
