Before you come in

Three things make the visit go faster.

Bring your medication list

Including supplements and anything prescribed elsewhere. Photographs of the bottles are fine.

Arrive ten minutes early

Paperwork at the front desk takes about that long for a first visit, less afterwards.

Know your pharmacy

Name and cross streets are enough. We send prescriptions electronically before you leave.

Insurance card

We verify coverage at check-in. If you are paying out of pocket, ask for the self-pay rate.
